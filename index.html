3. Design Constraints & Verification SPEC LIMITS
Constraints establish the operational & spatial boundaries. Dimensions are mechanically derived from Torque & Material properties, then verified against these constraints.
1. Max Shaft Diameter (D_max) SPATIAL ENVELOPE INPUT
Specified Boundary: 150.0 mm
Mechanically Calculated: 85.0 mm
Spatial Check: D ≤ D_MAX (PASS)
Mechanical Dependency: d = [(16·T) / (π·τ_allow)]^(1/3). Derived from applied torque T and material yield strength Syt (via τ_allow = Syt / 2FoS).
2. Max Allowable Twist (θ_max) RIGIDITY LIMIT (ISO/DIN)
Allowable Rigidity Limit: 1.000° / m
Mechanically Calculated: 0.878° / m
Rigidity Check: θ ≤ θ_MAX (RIGID)
Mechanical Dependency: θ = (T·L) / (J·G) · (180/π). Derived from torque T and material shear modulus G. If θ > θ_max, shaft is mechanically upsized for stiffness: D = [(32·T·L)/(π·G·θ_max)]^(1/4).
Standard Design Code Stated Limits:
• Standard Shaft Test Span: L_shaft = 1000 mm (1 meter)
• Cast Iron Allowable Shear Limit: τ_ci = 15.0 MPa
• Industry Rigidity Limit: θ ≤ 1.0° per meter span
Are Constraints Inputs or Mechanically Calculated?
  • Inputs (Boundary Limits): D_max is a geometric packaging constraint (housing/clearance limit). θ_max is an operational standard (vibration/deflection limit).
  • Mechanically Calculated: The actual shaft diameter D and actual angle of twist θ are computed from applied Torque T and Material Properties (Syt, G).
  • Coupling Design Loop: If calculated θ > θ_max, D is automatically upsized for torsional rigidity. If final D > D_max, the chosen material cannot handle the torque in the available space → you must select a stronger steel grade.
